Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

This policy explains what data Tracking Video Raf Estate ("the Service"), operated by PT Raf Estate, Malang, Indonesia, collects when a TikTok account is connected, and how it is used.

Data we collect from TikTok

DataTikTok scopeWhy
Open ID, display name, avataruser.info.basicTo know which company account a video belongs to
Public video list: video ID, title/caption, link, publish time, views, likes, comments, sharesvideo.listTo record daily video performance in the company's internal report

We do not collect passwords, private messages, followers' personal data, location, or any data from people who did not connect their own account.

How the data is used and stored

  • Statistics are saved in a private Google Sheet owned by PT Raf Estate and are used only for internal performance reporting (for example: which upload time performs best).
  • Access tokens are stored in the private settings of that Google Sheet's script, never in the sheet cells, and are used only to read the statistics above.
  • We do not sell, rent or share the data with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

Retention and deletion

  • Video statistics are kept as long as they are needed for reporting.
  • You can revoke access at any time in the TikTok app (Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions). After revoking, no new data is collected.
  • To delete the stored data of your account, email us and we will remove it within 30 days.
