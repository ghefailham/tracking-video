Tracking Video Raf Estate

Internal video performance tracker for PT Raf Estate

What it does

Tracking Video Raf Estate is an internal analytics tool used by the marketing team of PT Raf Estate, a property company in Malang, Indonesia. It records the daily performance of videos published on the company's own social media accounts (TikTok, YouTube, Instagram and Facebook) in a private Google Sheet, so the team can see which videos, upload days and upload times perform best.

How it uses TikTok

  1. The owner of a company TikTok account signs in with Login Kit and approves access.
  2. The tool reads the account's basic profile (user.info.basic) and its list of public videos with their statistics — views, likes, comments and shares (video.list, Display API).
  3. The numbers are saved once a day into the company's private Google Sheet for reporting. Nothing is posted, edited or deleted on TikTok.

Only accounts owned by PT Raf Estate and its partner property brands are connected. The tool is not offered to the public.
